# Informed Consent Statement for UX Usability Testing

Name of Study: **Bank App**

**Purpose of the Study:**  
Thank you for participating in this usability testing session. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the usability and user experience of the Bank App. By observing how users interact with the app, we aim to identify areas for improvement to enhance its functionality, design, and overall user satisfaction.

This study is part of our ongoing commitment to provide a secure, efficient, and user-friendly banking experience. Your feedback will play a crucial role in shaping the future of the app.

**What You’ll Be Asked to Do:**

During this session, you will be asked to complete a series of tasks using the app, such as navigating features, accessing account information, or performing transactions. You will also be encouraged to share your thoughts aloud as you interact with the app. The session will last approximately 10 minutes.

**Confidentiality:**

Your participation is completely voluntary, and your identity will remain confidential. Any data collected during the session, including screen recordings, task completion times, and feedback, will be used solely for research purposes. No personal identifying information will be associated with the results of this study.

**Voluntary Participation:**

Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without any consequences or need to explain your decision. If you feel uncomfortable at any point, you may pause or stop the session.

**Recording**

We would like to record your test session if you agree. Recording helps us review your session and understand your feedback. Your input helps us improve our design. We will store the recording securely. Only the research team will view the recording. No sharing outside the research team. No name in any report. Recordings will be deleted once the research ends.

You are free to ask us to stop recording. You are welcome to join the test without a recording.

**Risks and Benefits:**

There are no foreseeable risks associated with your participation in this study. While you will not receive direct compensation, your input will contribute significantly to improving the Bank App, which may benefit you as a user in the future.
