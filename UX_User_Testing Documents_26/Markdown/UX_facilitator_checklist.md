# UX Facilitator Checklist

## 1\. Welcome & Introduction

  - Greet participants and thank them for their time.

  - Explain purpose: ***'We’re testing the design, not you—there are no right or wrong answers.'***

  - Give session overview: tasks, think-aloud, recording, observers.

  - Reconfirm informed consent and confidentiality.

  - Ask if they have any questions before starting.

## 2. Observer & Recording Transparency

  - Inform participant: 'This session will be recorded for research purposes only.'

  - Inform participant: 'Observers may be watching remotely, they won’t interact with you.'

  - Confirm participant is comfortable and remind them they can stop anytime.

## 3. Think-Aloud Instructions

Introduce***: 'Please say out loud what you’re thinking, what you’re looking for, and any confusion.'***

If silent, use neutral prompts: ***'What are you thinking right now?', 'What made you choose that option?'***

## 4. Task Delivery Best Practices

  - Give one task at a time.

  - Frame tasks as realistic scenarios, not step-by-step instructions. Example***: 'Imagine you need to pay your electricity bill online. Show me how you’d do that.'***

  - Avoid hints or leading questions.

  - Encourage honesty: ***'If something feels confusing or frustrating, please tell us.'***

## 5. Wrap-Up

  - Thank the participant for their time.

  - Ask for overall impressions.

  - Remind them how their data will be used and stored.

  - Provide contact info for follow-up questions.
