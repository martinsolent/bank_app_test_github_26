# Think Aloud in User Testing – Introduction Task

*Computing | Southampton Solent University*

## Overview

Thinking Out Loud is a user testing method where a participant completes tasks while speaking their thoughts. You hear what they notice, what they look for, and where they struggle.

### Key points

- The participant says what they think as they work
- You listen without giving help
- You note confusion, pauses, and errors
- You collect quotes that show how they understand the design

This simple approach helps you see problems that are hidden during silent use and supports clear design improvements. You will find it useful in many testing sessions.

## Task

**Goal:** Help you practise Think Aloud during a short usability study.

### Setup

- Work in pairs
- Pick a simple website or app, for example a curriculum page or online store
- One person takes the role of participant
- One person takes the role of observer

### Participant steps

Complete three clear tasks, for example:

- Find contact details
- Add a product to a basket
- Change account settings

Talk through thoughts, decisions, and feelings:

- Speak out loud during each click or scroll

### Observer steps

Stay quiet and listen and take notes on:

- Words used
- Points of confusion
- Errors or pauses
- Suggestions

### Recording

- Write short quotes
- Mark time stamps if using screen recording software

### Debrief

- Swap roles and repeat
- Share findings with a small group
- Highlight one design issue
- Suggest one improvement

### Output

A short summary with:

- Goals
- Main issues
- One improvement recommendation
